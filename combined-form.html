THIS IS IMPORTANT INFORMATION PROVIDED BY BMW FINANCIAL SERVICES ABOUT THE COMMISSION WE PAY TO THE RETAILER.

Retailer: Ruxley BMW

What is the Retailer’s role?

The Retailer has an arrangement with us to introduce their customers to us. When they introduce you to us, they are acting as a “credit broker”. We will pay them a commission for this when you enter into an agreement with us. The Retailer will usually have a selection of lenders or intermediaries, but will normally send your details to us first.

When the Retailer introduces you to us, they are not acting independently. The commission amount might influence them. The Retailer is not giving you advice or recommendations. Depending on your situation, you may find a better deal if you look around.

What commission will we pay to the Retailer?

If you enter into the proposed finance agreement with us, we will pay the Retailer commission. We calculate commission in two different ways, depending on the vehicle being financed. The one that applies in your case is ticked and completed below.
