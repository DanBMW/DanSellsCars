Stock new car

Everything you need to know

You've chosen a car that's already in stock — now it goes through PDI (Pre-Delivery Inspection) and handover prep before it's yours. Here's what happens next and what you might be wondering.

How long until I get my car?

Typically 2–4 weeks from today, depending on how busy the workshop is. Once your signed order form and deposit are both confirmed, your car enters the PDI process — that's the Pre-Delivery Inspection where our technicians go through the car fully before we hand it over to you. Dan will confirm your expected delivery date within 24 hours of receiving your paperwork.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes — and Dan handles this for you. You'll need to provide your DVLA V317 transfer form along with your current V5C logbook (the registration document for the plate you're moving across). The DVLA transfer fee is charged before delivery. The important thing is to get this to Dan as early as possible — leaving it late can delay your handover date, so get in touch with him as soon as you can.

Can I order accessories now?

Yes. Genuine BMW accessories can still be added to your order at this stage. Some items — such as fitted floor mats, boot liners, or specific tech add-ons — need to be installed during the PDI window, so the sooner you let Dan know what you'd like, the better. Drop him a message and he'll get a quote together and confirm what's feasible in the timeframe.

Is driveaway insurance included?

No. You need to arrange your own fully comprehensive insurance policy before driving the car away on handover day. Make sure your policy start date and time covers you from the moment you take the keys — you will not be able to drive away without valid insurance in place. If you need a few days to sort this, just let Dan know and he'll work with you on timing.

When will I next hear from Dan?

Dan will be in touch within 24 hours of your signed order form and deposit being confirmed. He'll give you an expected delivery date and let you know if there's anything outstanding. After that, you'll hear from him again closer to handover to confirm the exact date and time and go through what you need to bring.

What about finance — what do I need to do?

If you're using BMW finance, the proposal form is your priority. Download it at the top of this page, complete all sections honestly, and email it back to Dan at daniel.cane@hedinautomotive.co.uk with the subject line "Finance proposal — [Your name]". The finance cannot be formally arranged until this is back with him. Once submitted, Dan will be in touch to confirm whether a formal application is ready to go.

What do I need to bring on handover day?

Dan will confirm the full list beforehand, but typically you'll need: a valid photo ID (driving licence or passport), proof of your insurance (on your phone or printed), and — if you have a part exchange — the V5C logbook for your current car plus both sets of keys. If your part exchange has any outstanding finance, bring your settlement figure confirmation too. Nothing should surprise you on the day — Dan will go through everything with you beforehand.

Factory order

Everything you need to know

You've placed a factory order — your BMW will be built exactly to your specification in Munich. This is how the majority of the best BMWs are bought, and Dan will keep you in the loop throughout the whole journey.

How long does a factory order take?

Factory orders typically take between 8 and 16 weeks from the point the order is placed, though this varies depending on the model, specification, and current production schedules. Dan will give you a production slot estimate at the time of ordering. Build slots are allocated by BMW in batches, so your exact timing depends on when your slot falls — but Dan will have this information and will share it with you as soon as it's confirmed.

Can I track my order?

There's no customer-facing tracking portal, but you're not left in the dark. Dan will update you at the key milestones: when your car enters production, when it leaves the factory, when it arrives in the UK, and again when it reaches Hedin Ruxley (usually around 4 weeks before your handover date). If you want an update at any point, just message Dan directly — he can check the system for you.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes — Dan handles this for you. You'll need to provide your DVLA V317 transfer form and your current V5C logbook (for the plate you're transferring). This needs to be with Dan before delivery day, so don't leave it until the last minute. The DVLA transfer fee is charged ahead of handover. Get in touch with Dan as soon as you're ready and he'll walk you through exactly what's needed.

Can I change my spec after the order is placed?

Sometimes — but only within a very short window, and it depends on whether BMW has allocated a build slot yet. Once the car enters production, no changes are possible at all. If you have any doubts or want to change anything, contact Dan immediately — even a small delay in raising it can mean missing the window entirely. The sooner you speak to him, the more likely something can be done.

Can I order accessories?

Yes. Genuine BMW accessories can be added at any point before handover. Some can be specified as part of the original factory order (which is often the most seamless way), and others can be fitted by our workshop during PDI prep when the car arrives. Let Dan know what you'd like and he'll advise on the best way to include them.

Is driveaway insurance included?

No. You need to arrange your own fully comprehensive insurance, active from the moment of handover. Make sure it covers the exact make, model and registration of your new car — Dan will confirm the final registration plate details ahead of time so you can set this up. You cannot drive the car away without valid insurance in place.

What do I need to do while I wait?

The most important things now are: return your signed order form if you haven't already, and if you're on finance, complete and email your finance proposal form to Dan at daniel.cane@hedinautomotive.co.uk. Beyond that, enjoy the wait — Dan will be in touch at each milestone. Roughly 4 weeks before your handover date, he'll reach out to confirm your collection slot, discuss what you need to bring, and make sure everything is in order for a smooth handover day.

BMW Approved Used

Everything you need to know

You've bought a BMW Approved Used vehicle — a pre-owned car that's been through BMW's certification process. Here's what that means for you, and what happens between now and collection.

When can I collect my car?

You can collect your car any time between 48 hours and a maximum of 2 weeks from today. The 48-hour minimum gives us time to complete any remaining prep work. The 2-week maximum exists to keep things moving — cars can't sit indefinitely once a purchase is agreed. Dan will be in touch within 24 hours to confirm your collection slot. If you need to collect sooner or have a specific day in mind, just let him know and he'll do his best to accommodate you.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes, you can — but for approved used vehicles, this is something you handle directly with the DVLA rather than through us. You'll need to apply using the DVLA's V317 form, which covers the transfer of a private number plate onto a vehicle. Dan can explain the process and point you to the right forms, but the dealer does not arrange private plate transfers on used cars — that's between you and the DVLA. It's worth doing this after you've received your new V5C for the car, which will come from the DVLA in the post.

Can I order accessories?

Yes. Genuine BMW accessories are still available for approved used vehicles. Speak to Dan as early as possible — some accessories need to be fitted before your collection date, so the more notice we have, the better. He can put together a quote and confirm what can be done in the available timeframe.

Is driveaway insurance included?

No. You must have your own fully comprehensive insurance confirmed and active before you drive the car away. This is non-negotiable — you cannot drive without valid insurance in place. If you're sorting insurance in advance, just make sure the policy covers the exact make, model and registration of the car you're collecting. Dan can confirm the registration details for you ahead of time.

What comes with a BMW Approved Used car?

Every BMW Approved Used vehicle has been through a thorough multipoint inspection by our trained technicians. Your car comes with a minimum 12-month BMW warranty (covering mechanical and electrical components), BMW Roadside Assistance, and an MOT where required. Dan can provide the full vehicle inspection report for your specific car — just ask and he'll send it over. You should also receive a service history with the car on collection day.

What do I need to bring on collection day?

Bring a valid photo ID (driving licence or passport), proof of your insurance on your phone or printed, and — if you have a part exchange — the V5C logbook for your current car plus both sets of keys. If your part exchange has any outstanding finance, bring your settlement figure confirmation too. Dan will confirm anything specific to your purchase in advance, so you won't arrive unprepared.

It's not too late

Protect your investment

BMW protection products can be added up to an absolute maximum of 30 days after purchase — but we strongly recommend sorting this before you collect. Once the car is on your driveway, some products become significantly harder (or impossible) to arrange.

Speak to Dan about any of the products below — it's a five-minute conversation that can save you thousands later.

🛡️

Paint Protection Film

A near-invisible film that protects your paintwork from stone chips, road debris, and light scratches. Self-healing in the sun. Best applied before the car leaves us — it's a very different job once it's been driven.

Best before pickup
📉

GAP Insurance

Covers the difference between what your insurer pays out and what you originally paid (or what you owe on finance) if your car is written off or stolen. Particularly valuable in the first couple of years of ownership.

Up to 30 days after purchase
🔧

Tyre & Alloy Cover

Covers the cost of repairing or replacing tyres and alloy wheels damaged by road hazards — potholes, kerbs, and the like. A single alloy repair can cost £100+, and a tyre replacement more. This pays for itself quickly.

Up to 30 days after purchase
✨

BMW Cosmetic Insurance

Covers minor cosmetic damage — small dents, door dings, light scratches to paintwork and interior. The kind of everyday damage that adds up quickly but doesn't meet the threshold for a standard insurance claim.

Up to 30 days after purchase
