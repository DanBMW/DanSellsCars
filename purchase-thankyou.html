Stock new car

Your specific next steps

You've chosen a car that's in stock and going through PDI (Pre-Delivery Inspection) and handover prep. Here's what's unique to your purchase. Answers that apply to everyone are in the sections further down the page.

How long until I get my car?

Approximately 2 weeks from today. Our handover specialist will be in touch within 7 days to schedule your appointment and confirm an exact date and time that works for you.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes, Dan handles this for you. You'll need to provide your DVLA V317 transfer form along with your current V5C logbook (the registration document for the plate you're moving across). The DVLA transfer fee is charged before delivery. Get this to Dan as early as possible, as leaving it late can push back your handover date.

Will I know my new registration number before I collect?

Dan will let you know your new registration number as soon as it is confirmed. However, you cannot use the registration number alone to insure the car. Your insurer will need the registration number together with the full vehicle description, which you can find on your order form. Without both, the car cannot be correctly identified on a policy.

Bear in mind that the DVLA typically does not update their records to reflect the new registration until approximately 5 days after collection. This is normal and will not affect your ability to drive the car, but it is worth knowing if you run any checks during that window.

Can I order accessories now?

Yes. Genuine BMW accessories can still be added to your order at this stage. Some items need to be fitted during the PDI window, so the sooner you let Dan know what you'd like, the better. Drop him a message and he'll get a quote together and confirm what's achievable in the timeframe. Browse BMW accessories →

Will my part exchange valuation still be valid when I collect?

Yes, your agreed valuation stands. Because the gap between buying a stock car and collecting it is relatively short, we don't revalue your part exchange unless an unusually long period of time passes. If anything changes or you have concerns, just speak to Dan directly.

What happens to my finance settlement when I part exchange?

If your part exchange car is on finance, your lender will issue a settlement figure. It is important to read this carefully, because settlement figures can be quoted either including or excluding your next scheduled payment. If the figure excludes your next payment, that payment may still be collected from you by your lender — this is normal, not an error.

Once the finance is settled, if the total received by your lender exceeds the outstanding balance, any overpaid amount will be returned to you. Finance companies typically refund overpayments within 14 days. If you are unsure whether your settlement figure includes or excludes your next payment, check your settlement letter or call your lender directly.

What do I need to bring on collection day?

Bring a valid photo ID (driving licence or passport) and proof of your insurance. If you have a part exchange, bring the V5C logbook for your current car plus both sets of keys. If that car has outstanding finance, bring your settlement figure confirmation too. If your finance agreement involves a borrowed amount over £60,000, you will need to sign your documents in person and bring a valid photo ID and proof of address. Your handover specialist will confirm the full list before the day.

Factory order

Your specific next steps

Your BMW will be built exactly to your specification. Dan will keep you updated at every milestone. Here's what's unique to a factory order. General answers that apply to everyone are in the sections further down.

How long does a factory order take?

Build times vary depending on the model and specification. Dan will have given you an estimated production slot when you placed the order, and will update you as soon as the schedule is confirmed by BMW. He'll be in touch within 7 days with your first update, and will keep you informed at each key milestone throughout the process.

Can I track my order?

There's no customer-facing tracking portal, but you won't be left wondering. Dan will update you when your car enters production, when it leaves the factory, when it arrives in the UK, and again when it reaches Hedin Ruxley. If you'd like a status check at any point, just message Dan and he can look it up for you.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes, Dan handles this for you. You'll need to provide your DVLA V317 transfer form and your current V5C logbook for the plate you're transferring. Get this to Dan well ahead of your handover date so nothing delays the day. The DVLA transfer fee is charged before delivery.

Can I change my spec after the order is placed?

Only within a very short window, and only if a build slot hasn't yet been allocated. Once the car enters production, no changes are possible at all. If you want to change anything, contact Dan immediately. Even a short delay could mean missing the window entirely.

When will I hear from someone?

Dan will be in touch within 7 days of your order being placed with an initial update. After that, you'll hear from him at each production milestone. Our handover specialist will contact you when the car is approaching its handover date to schedule your collection appointment and talk through what you need to bring.

Will my part exchange valuation still be valid when I collect?

This depends on your situation. If your current car is on finance, we will equity lock your equity. This means the equity figure agreed at the time of your order is protected throughout the build period, regardless of how long the wait is. If your car is owned outright, we will revalue it approximately one week before your handover date to ensure we're working with an up-to-date figure.

Finance companies typically refund overpayments within 14 days. If you are unsure whether your settlement figure includes or excludes your next payment, check your settlement letter or call your lender directly.

Bring a valid photo ID, proof of your insurance, and, if you have a part exchange, the V5C logbook and both sets of keys for your current car. If there's outstanding finance on your part exchange, bring your settlement figure confirmation too. If your finance agreement involves a borrowed amount over £60,000, you will need to sign your documents in person and bring a valid photo ID and proof of address. Your handover specialist will confirm the full list closer to the time.

BMW Approved Used

Your specific next steps

You've bought a BMW Approved Used vehicle, certified by BMW and prepared by our team. Here's what's specific to your purchase. General answers that apply to all buyers are in the sections below.

When can I collect my car?

You can collect any time between 48 hours and a maximum of 2 weeks from today. The 48-hour minimum gives us time to complete any remaining preparation, including a multipoint inspection (PDI) and any final detailing. Our handover specialist will be in touch within 48 hours to schedule your collection slot. If you have a specific date in mind, let Dan know and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Can I put my personal registration on it?

Yes, you can. For approved used vehicles, this is handled directly between you and the DVLA rather than through us. You'll need the DVLA V317 form to transfer your plate. Dan can walk you through the process and point you to the right paperwork, but the dealer doesn't arrange private plate transfers on used cars. It's best to do this once you've received your new V5C from the DVLA, which arrives by post within about a week of collection.

Can I order accessories?

Yes. Genuine BMW accessories are still available. Speak to Dan as soon as you can, as some accessories need to be fitted before your collection date and the more notice we have the better. He'll get a quote together and confirm what can be done in the timeframe. Browse BMW accessories →

When will I hear from someone?

Our handover specialist will contact you within 48 hours of your purchase to schedule your collection appointment and talk through everything you need to bring. After that, Dan will check in with you every 6 months throughout your ownership.

Will my part exchange valuation still be valid when I collect?

Yes, your agreed valuation stands. Because collection happens within a relatively short window (up to 2 weeks), we don't revalue unless an unusual amount of time passes. If you have any concerns, just speak to Dan.

Every BMW Approved Used vehicle comes with a minimum 12-month MOT, a minimum 12-month BMW warranty covering mechanical and electrical components, and BMW Roadside Assistance. Dan can provide the full vehicle inspection report for your specific car. Just ask. You'll also receive the service history on collection day. Warranties can be extended. Questions everyone asks

These answers apply regardless of which type of BMW you've bought.

Before & on collection day

How long does handover take?

Honestly, it depends, and that's intentional. Every handover is different. If you'd like a full walkthrough of every feature and technology in your car, we can spend hours together making sure you leave completely confident. If you're experienced with BMWs and prefer to get on the road, we'll keep it focused. The paperwork side varies too. Signing documents ahead of collection day means we can spend your time on the car, not admin. As a rough guide, allow anywhere from 1 hour to 3 hours or more, but we work at your pace. This experience is yours.

Should I set up the My BMW app before I come in?

Yes please. Download and register for the My BMW app ahead of your visit. Having it set up in advance means we can connect you to your new car much faster on the day.

Let Dan know you're all set

How many keys do I get?

If your car has Comfort Access (keyless entry — all BMWs are keyless start as standard), it will come with 1 key in most cases. Cars without Comfort Access come with 2 keys.

Do I need to sort my own insurance?

We offer 5 days' driveaway insurance to get you covered from the moment of collection. Setting this up is your responsibility, so please get in touch if you'd like the details and we'll talk you through it.

Your documents & tax

When will I get my V5C logbook?

Your V5C will be posted to you directly by the DVLA, and you should receive it within about a week of collecting your car, whether new or used. There's nothing you need to do; it will arrive automatically.

Is the car taxed?

New cars include the first year's road tax, so you're all set to drive away. For used cars, taxing the vehicle is the new owner's responsibility, but don't worry, we'll sort this with you during handover so you leave fully road-legal.

What about MOT and warranty?

Every car we sell comes with at least 12 months' MOT and 12 months' warranty as standard. New cars come with a 3-year manufacturer warranty. All warranties can be extended. Just ask Dan if you'd like to know more.

Some of my documents mention "Stephen James Ltd" — is that right?

Yes, completely normal. Stephen James Ltd was our previous company name before we became part of the Hedin Automotive Group. Some documents, system-generated letters, and finance paperwork may still carry the Stephen James name while the changeover is completed. It does not affect your purchase, your finance agreement, or any of your consumer rights in any way. If you have any concerns about a specific document, just ask Dan and he'll clarify.

Electric vehicles

How do I charge my electric vehicle?

Electric vehicles come supplied with a cable to plug into an untethered charge box. They do not include a 3-pin plug cable unless this was specifically ordered. We can refer you to Pod Point for your home charging setup, and you'll get a saving through our referral.

How does the Pod Point referral work?

Once we refer you, Pod Point will contact you directly to get things moving — but you don't have to wait for them. You're welcome to reach out to Pod Point yourself and let them know you've been referred by BMW, which can help speed things along.

You'll receive a confirmation email letting you know the referral has been made, so keep an eye out for that. Lead times can vary quite a bit — we've seen installations happen in as little as a week, and others take up to six weeks depending on demand and your location. With that in mind, the sooner you get in touch with Pod Point, the better.

Finance & payments

When do I sign my finance documents?

Finance documents are sent close to the end of the process and will come through for signing within one week of your handover date. Keep an eye out for emails from ShareDocs and Hyland Cloud. That's where your documents will appear. Signing them ahead of collection day means we can spend your time on the car rather than paperwork.

⚠️
Borrowing over £60,000?

If your finance agreement involves a borrowed amount over £60,000, you will need to come into the dealership in person to sign your documents. Please bring a valid photo ID and a recent proof of address. Dan will confirm whether this applies to you ahead of your handover date.

Who do I contact about my finance after purchase?

We're a broker rather than a lender, so once your finance is in place your agreement sits with your chosen lender. For anything after purchase, such as overpayments, changes to your agreement, settlement figures, or end-of-agreement options, please contact your lender directly as they'll have everything to hand.

How do I pay the remainder of my deposit / pay for my car?

We accept bank transfers and card payments. A few things worth knowing:

  • Please make your bank aware of the payment in advance so it isn't delayed or blocked.
  • Bank transfers should be completed before collection day so we can confirm safe receipt.
  • We accept a maximum of £2,000 per vehicle on a credit card.
  • We're unable to accept American Express.

After you drive away

What if something isn't quite right after I drive away?

If you notice any issue with your car after driving away, please contact Dan in the first instance and he'll advise you on the best way forward. We're here to help and want to make sure everything is exactly as it should be.

How do I raise a complaint?

For any concern about your vehicle, your handover experience, or anything else related to your purchase, please contact Dan directly in the first instance. Most things can be resolved quickly with a conversation, and that will always be the fastest route to a resolution.

For matters relating to regulated credit-broking or finance, Hedin Automotive Group operates a formal complaints process. If you raise a complaint and remain dissatisfied with the outcome, you may be entitled to refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service, an independent body that can investigate disputes free of charge. More information is available at financial-ombudsman.org.uk.

Will I hear from you again after collection?

Absolutely. We don't disappear after handover. Dan will check in with you roughly every 6 months throughout your ownership, and when the time is right we'll be on hand to help you into your next car, making the whole process as easy as it was this time.

Protect your investment

BMW protection products can be added up to an absolute maximum of 30 days after purchase. We strongly recommend sorting this before you collect, as once the car is on your driveway, some products become significantly harder (or impossible) to arrange.

Speak to Dan about any of the products below. It's a five-minute conversation that can save you thousands later.

Paint Protection Film

A near-invisible film that protects your paintwork from stone chips, road debris, and light scratches. Self-healing in the sun. Best applied before the car leaves us; it's a very different job once it's been driven.
