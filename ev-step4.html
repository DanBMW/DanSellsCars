Outstanding finance

Your settlement figure is the amount left to clear the finance and end the agreement today — it's not the same as your remaining balance. Here's how to get it:

  • In your finance provider's app — most show a live settlement or early settlement figure instantly.
  • By phone — call your lender and ask for a “settlement figure.” They'll give you one that's valid for a set number of days.
  • BMW or MINI Financial Services? Open the My BMW (or MINI) app, or call BMW Financial Services — the settlement figure is shown right there.

Can't get it right now? Pick “settlement figure unknown” above and I'll help you work it out before we speak.
