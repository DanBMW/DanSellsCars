Why this one

The estate that stopped apologising.

The 530e Touring is the sensible answer that does not feel like one. There are 570 litres behind the back seats before you fold anything, which swallows the pushchair, the dog crate or the golf clubs without a conversation about it.

It is a plug-in hybrid, so the school run, the commute and the supermarket happen on electricity, and the moment you point it at a motorway the petrol engine takes over without you thinking about charge stops. For a lot of people that is the whole argument for a PHEV in one sentence.

Inside, the BMW Curved Display runs the car through voice, touch or gesture, and the M Sport specification brings the styling, the seats and the wheels to match. Come and sit in one at Ruxley before you decide - it takes ten minutes and it settles the question.
