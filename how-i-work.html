How I work

No scripts.
Just a proper conversation.

If you have ever taken a breath and braced yourself before walking into a car showroom, this one is for you. Here is exactly what happens when you get in touch with me - and what never will.

01 · The first conversation

It starts with listening.

A first chat with me sounds nothing like a sales call. I want to know how you actually use a car - the school run, the motorway slog, the dog in the boot - who else drives it, what you love about the one you have and what has started to wind you up about it. Honestly, you will do most of the talking, and that is the point.

What I will never do is open with a monthly figure, nudge you toward whatever needs shifting off the forecourt this week, or read down a script someone handed me. There is no script. I listen, I go away and do the legwork, and I come back with a couple of options that actually fit what you told me.

02 · Why my opinion is worth having

Judgement earned behind the wheel.

Cars have been my thing since long before they paid the bills. I have spent years around motorsport and autotesting, where you learn fast what a car is really doing beneath you and why one spec sings while another leaves you cold. That is not something you can pick up from a brochure.

I also do proper mileage - 25 to 30,000 a year - and plenty of it now in electric. It has worked brilliantly for me, so the things people quietly worry about, like range on a cold morning or charging on a long trip, I can talk through from real driving rather than a training slide. When I give you a steer on a car, it comes from genuinely living with them.

03 · The pace

Your journey runs on your clock.

Some people go from first message to handover in a fortnight. Others chat with me on and off for a year before anything moves. Both are genuinely fine by me. You will not get a string of "just checking in!" messages, and going quiet for a month does not land you on some chase-up list.

Every questionnaire on this site can be paused, picked up later or ignored altogether, and nothing reaches me until you decide to send it. Come back whenever you like and carry on where you left off - I will still have your notes.

04 · The information

Everything on the table, in plain English.

Car finance comes with its own vocabulary, and it is easy to feel like that vocabulary is being used at you rather than for you. I would rather just translate it. PCP, contract hire, balloon payments, excess mileage - I will tell you what each one really means for your situation, including the bits that do not flatter the deal.

Here is where I draw the line, and I think it matters: my job is to help you understand your options so the decision is yours to make, clearly and confidently. I am not here to tell you what is "best for you" - that call belongs to you, and where it reaches into your wider finances, to an independent adviser. What I will always make sure of is that you are never deciding on half the picture.

05 · The team

One familiar face, a strong team behind it.

Here is something I am proud of rather than shy about: buying a car properly takes more than one pair of hands, and I have a brilliant team around me to make sure every step is done right. Technicians who prepare your car, a handover team who present it beautifully, a service department who look after it for years, and the people who keep the paperwork spot on. That is what a proper dealership is for, and it is a genuinely good thing for you.

What you get is the best of both. One consistent person - me - who knows your whole story and carries it the whole way through, with a strong team assisting at every step. If something ever is not quite right, you do not have to work out whose department it belongs to. You just tell me, and I will sort it.

06 · Afterwards

Handover is the start, not the finish.

Handover day is my favourite part of the job, but it is not where things end. Months down the line, when a warning light pops up, or you cannot for the life of you find a setting, or you are just wondering what your car might be worth against something newer - message me. Plenty of people who bought from me years ago still do, and I would not have it any other way.

No hard sell to finish on, either. Whenever it is useful to talk, you know exactly where I am.
