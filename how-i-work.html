How I work

No scripts.
Just a proper conversation.

If you have ever braced yourself before walking into a car showroom, this page is for you. Here is what actually happens when you get in touch - and what never will.

01 · The first conversation

It starts with listening.

A first conversation with me usually sounds nothing like a sales call. I will ask how you use a car day to day, who travels in it, what you like about the one you have and what has started to annoy you about it. You will probably do most of the talking.

What I will not do is open with a monthly figure, steer you towards whatever is on the forecourt this week, or work through a checklist someone gave me. There is no checklist. I take notes, I go away, and I come back with options that fit what you actually said.

02 · Why my opinion is worth having

Judgement earned outside the showroom.

Cars have been my thing since long before they were my job. I have spent years around motorsport and autotesting, where you learn quickly what a car is really doing beneath you and why one specification drives beautifully while another disappoints. And I run an electric BMW as my own daily car, so the questions people worry about - range in January, charging on a trip, what the running costs honestly look like - I answer from my own life, not a training slide.

That experience is the reason customers tend to trust my judgement on cars. It is guidance about metal, not money: I am not a financial adviser and nothing I say is financial advice. When it comes to the numbers, my job is to lay them out clearly and completely, and then get out of your way.

03 · The pace

Your journey runs on your clock.

Some people go from first message to handover in a fortnight. Others talk to me on and off for a year before anything moves. Both are fine, and I genuinely mean that. You will never get a "just checking in!" barrage from me, and a quiet month does not put you on some lapsed-lead list.

Every questionnaire on this site can be paused, resumed or abandoned, and nothing reaches me until you choose to send it. When you are ready, pick the conversation back up exactly where we left it - I will still have the notes.

04 · The information

Everything on the table, in plain English.

Car finance has its own vocabulary, and it is easy to feel that the vocabulary is being used at you. I would rather translate it. PCP, contract hire, balloon payments, excess mileage - I will explain what each one actually means for you, including the parts that are less flattering to the deal.

The line I hold is simple: I help you understand your options so you can make your own call. I will not tell you what is "best for you" - that is a decision that belongs to you, and where it touches your wider finances, to a properly authorised independent adviser. What I can promise is that you will never make it on half the picture.

05 · The team

One consistent contact. Not a one-man band.

I used to be tempted to say "you deal with me and only me", because it sounds reassuring. It is also not quite honest, and you deserve the honest version: a car bought properly involves more hands than mine. Technicians prepare it, a handover team presents it, the service department keeps it right for years, and administrators make the paperwork exact.

The truthful promise is better than the flattering one. You have one consistent person - me - who knows your whole story, carries it through every stage, and is answerable to you for the lot. Behind that, a properly resourced dealership doing what a good dealership should. If something is not right, you do not have to work out whose department it is. You just tell me.

06 · Afterwards

Handover is the start, not the finish.

The day you collect the car is the best part of my job, but it is not the end of it. Months later, when a warning light appears or you cannot find a setting or you are wondering what the car might be worth against something newer, message me. The people who bought from me years ago still do, and I like it that way.

No hard close here, either. When it is useful to talk, you know where I am.
