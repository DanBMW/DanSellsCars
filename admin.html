Everything on the office board, in one place. Changes land on every screen showing it within a second - nobody has to walk over to the TV.

Connecting to the board…

What’s on the wall

Hold every screen on one board, or leave it cycling. A hold lapses back to the rotation after fifteen minutes, so nobody can leave the wall stuck on one screen all afternoon.

The rotation

Which screens the wall cycles through. Switching one off takes it off the wall straight away, and a slot with nothing in it is skipped anyway.

A birthday card, the mug shot ask and the forecourt list take half of this, up to fifteen seconds - they are a glance, not a read.

Scenes and stunts

The things that interrupt the board. One plays at a time, in turn, so switching some off makes the rest come round more often rather than leaving gaps.

Play a sketch now

Takes the floor on every screen showing the board, whatever is up.

Targets

The figures the board is measured against. Changing the profit target changes the title, the track, the pace line and the champion scene together.

Mug shot of the week

The team vote, and the winner takes a slot in the rotation for the week. This is where you overrule that - put a different one up, or take one down if it should not be on a wall. A blocked picture disappears from the voting page too and cannot win a later week.

Every entry

Nothing uploaded.

Banner along the bottom

Scrolls along the bottom of every screen, over whatever else is up. Keep each line short - it goes past about once a minute, and three at once means nobody reads any of them.

Screenshots on the board

The managers put these up themselves. This is here so you can take one down without going and finding whoever did it.

Birthdays

A card stays in the rotation for a week from the day it goes up, then drops out on its own. Take one down early here.
