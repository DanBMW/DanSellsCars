How to play

🕹️ Move

Phone: drag the right side of the screen — a joystick appears under your thumb. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys.

👋 Interact

Walk up to anything and hit the blue action button (bottom-left) — or tap the left side of the screen, or press E / Space. Talk to customers, use the coffee machine, shoo pigeons…

🚗 Drive

You can get in any forecourt car! Push the direction you want to go — the car turns and follows. Managers will ask you to move cars between bays, and hot customers may want a test drive round the block — hit every checkpoint and don't prang it.

📊 Survive the day

⚡ Energy drains — refill at the coffee machine. 🚻 Bladder fills — use the toilet. 😤 Composure takes hits — recover on the sofa. Max one out = you crash and lose points.

🎯 Win

Hit the deal target to pass the day and unlock the next one. Your best score on each day adds up to your career total — top career total wins the £10.
