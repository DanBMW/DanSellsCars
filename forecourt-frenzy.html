How to Play

  • Move: Tap the floor to walk. Tap a customer, car or station to walk over and interact.
  • Customers: 4 questions decide the deal. Reach them before the patience ring empties. Type is hidden — read the conversation.
  • Service walk-ins: Some only want Service → send them to Matt fast. But if they mention buying, engage the sale first or you'll lose it.
  • Phone & email: Pop up mid-shift. The world keeps moving while you deal with them.
  • Car jobs: Will (forecourt) and Serge (showroom) ask you to move cars. Tap the highlighted car to pick it up, then tap the green bay to drop it — beat the timer.
  • Bars: ⚡ Energy drains (refill: ☕ coffee). 🚻 Bladder fills (clear: 🚻 toilet). 😤 Patience takes hits (restore: 🛋️ sofa). Max one out = crash.
  • Target: 3 deals. Hit it with good customer handling for the full bonus.
