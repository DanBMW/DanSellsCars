Business benefits

Lower benefit in kind

Electric company cars can be very tax efficient compared with petrol or diesel, which can make a big difference to directors, sales teams, and fleet users.

Less downtime

EVs typically need less routine servicing because there are fewer moving parts, helping keep busy drivers and vehicles on the road.

Cleaner image

Quiet, low emission driving helps with customer perception, sustainability goals, and access to low emission zones.

For business users, the right EV can improve cash flow, reduce maintenance hassle, and make the monthly package easier to predict. If you are choosing for a company car or director car, it is worth comparing the real world monthly total rather than only the list price.
