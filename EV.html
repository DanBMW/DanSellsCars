Business tax

Tax summary for new electric vehicles.

For new electric BMWs bought through a business, the headline point is straightforward: 100% first-year allowance can apply to qualifying expenditure, and EV chargepoints may also qualify. Leases are treated separately, so an accountant should confirm the exact treatment for your business.

Purchase

Buying a new EV through the business may allow first-year allowance on qualifying expenditure. Confirm the timing and your structure with your accountant.

Leasing

Leases are usually treated differently from purchases, so the tax effect should be checked before you commit.

Chargepoints

Qualifying EV chargepoints may also attract first-year allowance, subject to the current HMRC rules.

FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

Do I need home charging?

It helps, but it is not essential. The calculator lets you compare home, mixed and public charging so the saving is realistic.

Is the tax position fixed?

No. The summary is based on current UK guidance, but your accountant should confirm the exact result for your business and vehicle choice.

Which BMW should I look at first?

The i4 eDrive40 if you want the most emotional, driver-focused car. The iX1 if practicality is the priority.
