Business tax

Tax summary for new electric vehicles.

For new electric BMWs bought through a business, 100% first year allowance can apply to qualifying expenditure, and EV chargepoints may also qualify. Leases are treated separately, so an accountant should confirm the exact treatment for your business.

FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

Do I need home charging?

It helps, but it is not essential. The calculator lets you compare home, mixed and public charging so the saving is realistic.

Is the tax position fixed?

No. The summary is based on current UK guidance, but your accountant should confirm the exact result for your business and vehicle choice.

Which BMW should I look at first?

The i4 eDrive40 if you want the most emotional, driver focused car. The iX1 if practicality is the priority.
