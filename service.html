Hi, I'm Dan. If your BMW is booked in with the service team, that is honestly a good time for us to grab ten minutes together. No rushing, no pressure. Some people walk away wanting to make a change. Plenty don't. Both are completely fine.

Worth knowing before you hand the keys over: every service, every MOT and every set of tyres nudges a car's value down, and a lot of customers are surprised how strong their BMW's value is right now. If the timing turns out to be right, a change can be more straightforward than you would think. If it is not, I will say so.

Pop your reg in below - it takes about 45 seconds - and I will have an honest figure ready for when we speak, based on real market knowledge rather than a computer algorithm. There are no forms about your own car; I will see it in person while it is here.
