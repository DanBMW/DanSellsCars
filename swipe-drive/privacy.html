Privacy

How SwipeToDrive uses your details, in plain English.

Who looks after your details

SwipeToDrive is run by Dan Cane, a BMW sales executive in Sidcup, to help you find a car and get in touch with him. Dan works for Hedin Automotive Ltd, which is the data controller for the details you give here under UK data protection law. Dan handles your enquiry personally on its behalf.

What we collect

  • Your answers: budget, the kind of car you want, new or used, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Your swipes: the cars you like or pass on, so Dan knows what you are drawn to.
  • Contact details, only when you give them: your name and a mobile number or email address.
  • Analytics, only if you accept them: how the app is used, through Google Analytics. Nothing is collected until you tap Accept on the cookie bar.

You can browse and swipe without giving any contact details. Until you do, your answers and swipes stay on your own device.

How your details are used

  • To show you cars that match what you asked for.
  • To let Dan reply to you about the cars you liked, a test drive or anything you asked.
  • If you tick the box to hear about new stock, to tell you when a car that matches comes in. You can stop this at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp, or in SwipeToDrive under Me.

We rely on your consent for stock alerts and analytics, and on legitimate interests to answer an enquiry you started.

Who else handles them

  • Supabase: stores your SwipeToDrive account, answers and swipes once you save them.
  • Formspree: passes your enquiry to Dan by email.
  • Google Analytics: usage figures, only if you accept analytics.
  • Your enquiry may also be added to the dealership's own customer system so Dan can help you.

Your details are never sold or passed to anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep them

Enquiries are kept for as long as the dealership's retention policy requires, normally up to six years after any purchase, and then deleted or anonymised. You can ask Dan to delete your SwipeToDrive account at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to how they are used, or withdraw consent at any time. Message Dan on WhatsApp on 07827 138197 to do any of these. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

SwipeToDrive stores your answers, swipes and cookie choice on your device so the app works. Google Analytics cookies are only set if you tap Accept. Tap Cookie settings at the foot of the Me screen, or clear your browser data, to change your mind.
