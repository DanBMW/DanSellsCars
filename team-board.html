What's on the board

Hold the wall display on one board, or leave it cycling. Applies to every screen showing the board, and lapses back to the rotation after fifteen minutes.

Sketches

Plays on every screen showing the board, not just this one. Sketches run with the sound off.

Play sketches automaticallyOne roughly every ten minutes

Board control is not switched on in the database yet, so these play on this screen only. Publish the boardcontrol rules in Firebase to reach every screen.

Screenshot on the board

Anything you want the team to see - a stock list, a target sheet, a message. It joins the board rotation for thirty seconds at a time, with your caption above it.

Birthday

Plays on every screen straight away with the tune, then the card joins the rotation for a week. More than one at a time is fine - they each get their own slot. If they have their own clip it comes round with the card, about once an hour.

Every screen

Pushes a refresh to every screen showing the board, so a change does not need somebody walking over to the TV. They also reload themselves at 4am.
