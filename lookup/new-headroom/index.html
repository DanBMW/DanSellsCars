Internal only. Never show or send to a customer. Standard price, discount, floor and monthly at floor are estimates. The floor assumes a maximum of 10% off BMW standard price. Monthly at floor scales today's BMW Financial Services figure for the lower cash price on the same deposit, term, APR and final payment; it is not a quote. Get a real quote before talking numbers.

How standard price is worked out: a car on BMW standard price (locator flag off) uses its own price, confidence high. A retailer priced car uses the median price of standard priced cars of the same model and trim: medium with 2 or more of them, low with 1. Options differ between cars, so the gap mixes options with discount; a gap below zero shows as 0. No comparable means the current price is treated as standard (confidence none), so its headroom may be overstated.
