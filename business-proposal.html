How it works

Four steps to get your business finance moving

1

Open the form

Click the button above. The form opens in a new browser tab — no download required. You can type directly into every field.

2

Complete all sections

Work through each section: company details, bank information, trade details, and the signing director's personal, address, and employment information.

3

Save as PDF

Click the blue "Print / Save as PDF" button at the top of the form — or press Ctrl+P (Windows) / Cmd+P (Mac) — and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

4

Email it back to Dan

Attach the saved PDF and email it to Dan. Use the subject line: "Company Finance Proposal: [Company Name]"

Dan's email address

Once your completed form is received, Dan will be in touch to confirm next steps on your company finance application.

Your data & privacy. The company proposal form opens and runs entirely in your browser. No information you enter is transmitted to or stored on this website. The completed PDF is sent directly from your own email client to Dan and does not pass through any third-party service or database.
