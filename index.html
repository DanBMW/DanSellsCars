The promise

One name you know.
A proper team behind it.

When you buy a BMW through me, I am your point of contact. Not a rotating cast, not a shared inbox - me.

And I have a strong team around me to help at every step: technicians who prepare your car properly, a handover team who present it beautifully, and a service department who look after it for years to come. You get the best of both - one familiar face who knows your whole story, and a proper dealership behind that face doing what it does best.

What that means for you is simple: the thread never gets dropped. You always know who to speak to, your questions come to someone who already knows the back-story, and nothing gets passed around until it goes quiet. From your first message to long after you have driven away, that person is me.

No scripts. No pressure. Just straight answers, so you can make your own call.

A little about me

Cars are not just the day job.

I have been around cars my whole life. Weekends have meant motorsport and autotesting - the kind of driving that teaches you what a car is really doing underneath you, and what separates a good one from a great one. You cannot fake that sort of interest for forty hours a week, and customers can always tell.

I also cover serious miles - 25 to 30,000 a year - and a good chunk of that is now electric. It has worked brilliantly for me, so when you ask about real-world range, charging on a long run, or how the sums actually stack up after a few years, you get an answer from someone who lives it, not a line from a brochure.

What that means is straightforward: when I give you a view, it comes from genuine experience with the cars - and it is always offered, never pushed.
