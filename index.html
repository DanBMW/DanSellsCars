The promise

One name you know.
A proper team behind it.

When you buy a BMW through me, I am your point of contact. Not a rotating cast, not a shared inbox - me.

I will not pretend I do everything singlehandedly, because that would not be true and it would not be better. Behind me at Ruxley there is a handover team who prepare your car properly, a service department who look after it for years afterwards, and managers who back me when I need an answer quickly. That is what a well-resourced dealership is for.

What I promise is simpler, and I think more valuable: the thread never gets dropped. You always know who to speak to, your questions come to someone who knows your whole story, and nothing gets passed around until it goes quiet. From first message to long after handover, that person is me.

No scripts. No pressure. Plain information, handed over clearly, so you can make your own call.

A little about me

Cars are not just the day job.

I have been around cars my whole life. Weekends have meant motorsport and autotesting - the kind of driving that teaches you what a car is actually doing underneath you, and what separates a good one from a great one. You cannot fake that interest for forty hours a week; customers can always tell.

And I drive electric every day. Not as a company statement - as my own car, on my own drive, through my own winters. So when you ask about real-world range, or charging without a driveway, or what happens to the sums after four years, you get an answer from experience rather than a brochure.

None of that makes me a financial adviser, and I will never dress guidance up as advice. It just means that when I offer a view, it is an informed one - and it is offered, never imposed.
